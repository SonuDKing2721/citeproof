# No-cost Muse Glimmer inference route research

**Checked:** October 29, 2026
**Purpose:** Find a Muse Glimmer route that can be used without spending money, compare public GitHub model directories and inference marketplaces, and separate verified free use from hackathon bonus eligibility. This records public provider terms and model listings only; no account was created and no inference request was sent.

## Free alternatives for cross-model checks

These routes do not serve Muse Glimmer; they are options for a separately labeled baseline or app smoke check. Their outputs must not be mixed into Muse Glimmer's benchmark numbers.

| Route | Published free access | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OpenRouter free-model router | `openrouter/free` selects from the current free catalog; free variants are $0 and an unpaid account is limited to 50 requests/day and 20/minute. | The model and upstream provider can change between calls, and free model/provider data policies vary. Use only as a general functional check, not a matched-model comparison. |
| Google AI Studio / Gemini API | The official pricing page includes a free tier for selected models, including Gemini 3 Flash; API token pricing is $0 on eligible projects. | Quotas vary by model/project, some projects are not eligible, and free-tier content may be used to improve Google's products. Public issue and source text only. |
| Cloudflare Workers AI | The Workers Free plan includes 10,000 Neurons per day; selected models remain available on Free, including Gemma 4 and NVIDIA Nemotron. | Model-specific pricing/access matters: some newer models require Workers Paid. This is an alternative model family, not a Glimmer endpoint. |

## Recommendation

Keep NVIDIA as the current app route until a deliberate, no-cost change is made. Routeway is the strongest newly found exact-model route: its own catalog publishes `muse-glimmer-30b:free` at $0 with 200 daily requests on the free Starter plan. Before using it, verify signup does not require a payment method and check the account's free model quota; the upstream provider is undisclosed and may be substituted. Requesty remains another exact-model option, but it forwards to NVIDIA, so there is no evidence it will solve the observed timeouts. Vercel's $5 monthly free AI Gateway credit is a possible bounded Together fallback, but it is a credit pool, not a $0 model or indefinite free tier. Any trial should use only public issue/source text and must stop at the documented free allowance; never enable top-up or pay-as-you-go.

Requesty is not shown as an event inference partner. The route may support the general Meta-model contribution, but it is not verified for the event's open-weight bonus. Continue to omit that bonus claim until the accepted rules or an organizer confirms the exact provider/model route.
