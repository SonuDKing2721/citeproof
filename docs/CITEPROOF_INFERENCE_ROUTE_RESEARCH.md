**Checked:** October 29, 2026
**Purpose:** Find a Muse Glimmer route that can be used without spending money, compare public GitHub model directories and inference marketplaces, and separate verified free use from hackathon bonus eligibility. No account was created and no payment credentials or payment signatures were used. One minimal, no-auth BlockRun health request was attempted on October 29; it timed out after 45 seconds without a model response.

## Recommendation

Keep NVIDIA as the current app route until a deliberate, no-cost change is made. BlockRun is the easiest exact-model route found that advertises no signup, key, or payment method, but a minimal no-credential request timed out after 45 seconds. Its NVIDIA-labeled model listing does not establish a different serving upstream, so it is not a demonstrated reliability fix. Routeway remains the strongest candidate to investigate for a different upstream: it lists `muse-glimmer-30b:free` at $0 with 200 daily requests and documents the OpenAI-compatible API base URL, but signup requirements and upstream identity remain unverified. Requesty also forwards to NVIDIA, and its data-use terms make it less suitable. Vercel's $5 monthly free AI Gateway credit is a bounded Together fallback, not a $0 model or indefinite free tier. The current computer cannot self-host the 17 GB quantized model. Any future trial must use only public issue/source text and stop at the verified free allowance; never enable top-up or pay-as-you-go.

The participant-provided guide summary says Stage 1 accepts reasonable use of Meta's Model API and/or Meta open-weight models, while its open-weight bonus requires participant hardware or partner inference. The public event listing names Baseten, Modal, and Fireworks; neither NVIDIA, BlockRun, Routeway, nor Requesty is named there. The publicly accessible Meta and Cerebral Valley pages still say the Official Rules would be published before October 26, and neither page links the accepted rules. Cerebral Valley's event Markdown now says registration is closed. The accepted copy may only be visible to selected participants in the portal. Citeproof's Muse Glimmer usage appears to fit the Stage 1 model requirement based on the supplied summary, but its current route does not establish the open-weight bonus. Continue to omit any bonus claim pending accepted rules or organizer confirmation.
